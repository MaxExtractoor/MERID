## Risk Assessment

### Audit Risk Areas

**High Risk Areas**:
- **Risk Domain Implementation**: New domain with limited operational history
- **SLO Compliance**: Complex multi-domain SLO framework
- **Change Management**: Rapid development pace
- **Documentation Accuracy**: Dynamic system environment

**Medium Risk Areas**:
- **Incident Response**: Limited incident history
- **Data Quality**: Complex data flows
- **Security Controls**: Multi-domain access requirements
- **Performance Monitoring**: Complex system interactions

**Low Risk Areas**:
- **Backup & Recovery**: Established procedures
- **Access Control**: Well-defined roles
- **Documentation**: Comprehensive documentation framework
- **Training**: Established training programs

### Mitigation Strategies

**High Risk Mitigation**:
- **Risk Domain**: Extended shadow mode validation
- **SLO Compliance**: Detailed SLO validation testing
- **Change Management**: Enhanced change documentation
- **Documentation Accuracy**: Regular documentation reviews

**Medium Risk Mitigation**:
- **Incident Response**: Incident simulation exercises
- **Data Quality**: Enhanced data validation
- **Security Controls**: Regular security audits
- **Performance Monitoring**: Enhanced monitoring coverage
